How it works

Three steps. Zero effort.

1

Browse Amazon

Shop normally on any Amazon site — .nl, .de, .com, .co.uk, and more. ShopSwitch watches quietly in the background.

2

Automatic matching

ShopSwitch looks up the product using EAN barcodes, smart title matching, and bilingual search in English and Dutch.

3

Compare & switch

See match quality and prices across shops. Click to open the product on bol., Libris, or any matched store instantly.

Features

Smart, not just simple

🔍

EAN barcode matching

Uses universal product barcodes via UPCitemdb's 495M+ product database for precise matching — not just keyword guessing.

🏪

Multi-shop search

Searches multiple stores in parallel. Books are automatically checked on both bol. and Libris. More shops coming soon.

⚖️

Weighted matching

Brand names and specs like "64GB" or "500ml" get extra weight in scoring — so you see real matches, not random results.

⚠️

Spec diff detection

When quantities or sizes differ (e.g. 3-pack vs single), ShopSwitch flags the difference clearly instead of hiding it.

💰

Price comparison

Compare prices across all matched shops at a glance. See the exact difference in euros and percentage for each store.

🔒

Privacy first

No accounts, no tracking, no data collection. Everything runs locally in your browser. Fully open source.
