💙 Support development

Links include a referral code — costs you nothing
When enabled, links to bol. include a small referral tag. This helps fund ShopSwitch development at zero extra cost to you. You'll always see the same prices — we just get a tiny commission from bol. if you buy something. Thank you for your support!

🎨 Badge style

Green when cheaper, red when pricier on bol.
By default the badge shows a green checkmark whenever a product is found on bol. Enable this to colour the badge based on price: green when cheaper, red when more expensive than Amazon.
